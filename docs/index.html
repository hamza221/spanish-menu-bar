Spanish Menu Bar

For macOS

One Spanish word.
Every 30 minutes.

A tiny menu bar app that teaches you vocabulary while you work. Click the word to see what it means and hear how it sounds.

Download for Mac View source ›

Free and open source · macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon and Intel

Small app. Quietly effective.

⇄

Shuffled, never repeated

All 29,743 words come in a random order, and every word appears once before any word appears again. Press Next whenever you want another.

◉

Only counts when you’re there

A word counts as seen only if your Mac was awake and unlocked and you moved the mouse while it was showing. If you were away, the word stays put.

◷

Remembers what you’ve seen

Open a word you’ve met before to see when: Last time seen 15th of September 2026.

♪

Hear it spoken

Click the speaker or press ⌘P to hear the word in a natural Spanish voice built into macOS.

⌂

Private by design

Runs entirely offline in the App Sandbox. No accounts, no analytics, no network access.
