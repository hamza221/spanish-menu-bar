Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Short version

Spanish Menu Bar collects no data. Nothing leaves your Mac.

What the app stores

To avoid repeating words and to show when you last saw one, the app keeps a small file on your Mac. It contains the shuffled word order, how many times you have seen each word, and when you last saw it. The file is in the app’s sandbox container and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app and deleting ~/Library/Containers/com.hamzamahjoubi.SpanishMenuBar removes it.

Presence detection

A word counts as “seen” only if your Mac is awake and unlocked and the mouse moved recently. The app reads only the time since the last mouse movement, which macOS provides without special permissions. It does not record where the pointer went, what you clicked or what you typed.

Pronunciation

Words are spoken by the speech synthesizer built into macOS, on your Mac.

Network, analytics and third parties

The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
